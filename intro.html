AI Proxy Delegation Study

Welcome to the Study

Thank you for your interest in our research on workplace delegation.

Study Overview

This study investigates how individuals delegate tasks to autonomous proxies in professional workplace and daily settings. Specifically, you will evaluate delegating a specific task to two distinct agents: an AI Secretary and a Human Secretary.

When you delegate a task to your secretary (AI or human), they will plan the work, actually execute it, and complete it entirely on your behalf—acting under your name—without waiting for or asking for your final approval.

We want to understand what tasks people feel comfortable delegating, and how perceptions differ between an AI Secretary and a Human Secretary.

What to Expect

  • You will first answer a few brief background questions.
  • You will examine a specific workplace task scenario through a short visual dialogue sequence.
  • You will evaluate this task under two conditions: delegating to an AI Secretary and delegating to a Human Secretary.
  • Finally, you will answer a few brief concluding questions regarding your general attitudes.
  • The entire study takes approximately 5–7 minutes to complete.
