Introduction

This survey is about an AI Secretary, a new kind of AI system that does not stop at giving you suggestions. When you delegate a specific task to this AI Secretary, it will plan the work, actually execute it, and complete it entirely on your behalf—acting under your name—without waiting for or asking for your final approval.

We want to understand what kinds of tasks people are comfortable letting an AI Secretary handle completely on their own, and what makes some tasks feel more okay to delegate than others.

This survey is about working with a Human Secretary. When you delegate a specific task to this Human Secretary, they will plan the work, actually execute it, and complete it entirely on your behalf—acting under your name—without waiting for or asking for your final approval.

We want to understand what kinds of tasks people are comfortable letting a Human Secretary handle completely on their own, and what makes some tasks feel more okay to delegate than others.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are only interested in your personal opinions.
