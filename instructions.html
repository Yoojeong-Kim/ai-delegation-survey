Scenario Instructions

Please read carefully before starting the scenarios.

  • 1
    You will be presented with 4 different workplace scenarios.
  • 2
    For each scenario, you will first see an image representing your Point of View (POV) in that specific situation. Please look at it carefully as if it were a real situation in your workplace.
  • 3
    After viewing the image for 3 seconds, you will be asked whether you would delegate the task to your Secretary.
  • 4
    After making your choice, you will answer a few short questions about your thoughts on the scenario.
