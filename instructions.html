How It Works

Please read the following instructions carefully before beginning the scenarios.

1. Review the Scenario

You will be presented with a series of workplace scenarios. For each scenario, an image will appear on your screen for exactly 5 seconds. Please study it carefully.

2. Make a Decision

After viewing the image, you will be asked whether you want to delegate the task to your Secretary. You must choose either YES or NO.

3. Rate Your Agreement

Finally, you will rate your agreement with several statements regarding your decision, on a scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 7 (Strongly Agree).
