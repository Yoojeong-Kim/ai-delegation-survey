Step 1 of 3 — Instructions Reading

How It Works

Please read the following instructions carefully before beginning the scenarios.

1. Review the Workplace Scenario

You will be presented with a specific workplace scenario through a 3-scene visual dialogue sequence. Each scene will appear on your screen for a minimum of 5 seconds. Please review the delegated task, required information, and execution details carefully.

2. Two Condition Evaluations (AI & Human Secretary)

You will evaluate this exact task under two separate conditions: working with an AI Secretary and working with a Human Secretary (presented sequentially). For each condition, you will make an initial delegation decision: YES or NO.

3. Rate Your Agreement & Perspectives

Following your decision, you will rate your agreement with statements regarding your delegation intention, perceived risks, and perceived benefits on a scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 7 (Strongly Agree).
